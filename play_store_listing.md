## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Exhale is a minimalist voice recording application designed for emotional release and mindfulness. Record your thoughts, listen once, and let them disappear.

**Key Features:**
• Simple one-tap recording interface
• Real-time wave visualization during playback
• Automatic deletion after listening
• Complete privacy - no data storage or transmission
• Multilingual support in 13 languages
• Dark theme with calming design

**How It Works:**
1. Tap the microphone button to start recording
2. Speak your thoughts, concerns, or emotions
3. Tap again to stop recording
4. Listen to your recording with animated wave visualization
5. Recording automatically deletes when finished

**Privacy First:**
- All processing happens locally on your device
- No data collection or transmission
- No account creation required
- Recordings never leave your device
- Automatic cleanup of temporary files

**Perfect For:**
- Emotional expression and release

- Private journaling alternative
- Therapeutic self-expression

**Technical Features:**
- High-quality audio recording

Exhale provides a safe, private space for verbal expression without judgment or permanent storage. Use it whenever you need to voice your thoughts and let them go.

No subscriptions, no ads, no data collection - just a simple tool for mindful expression.

## Developer Response to Common Questions

**Q: Is my data safe?**
A: Yes, absolutely. Exhale does not collect, store, or transmit any data. All audio processing happens locally on your device and recordings are automatically deleted after playback.

**Q: Do you store recordings?**
A: No. Recordings are temporarily stored only during the session and are permanently deleted when playback ends.

**Q: Is there a subscription fee?**
A: No. Exhale is completely free with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

**Q: What languages are supported?**
A: Exhale supports 13 languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese, Russian, Arabic, Korean, Dutch, and Swedish.
